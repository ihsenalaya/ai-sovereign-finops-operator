\section{Conclusion}
The rebuilt AKS deployment now supports a clearer systems paper than the earlier partial draft. Real workloads, real Azure model endpoints, and real operator reconciliations demonstrate live FinOps attribution, quality-gate execution, budget-state progression, declared-residency findings, route actuation gated by quality evidence with human escalation, and shadow-AI detection. The central contribution of the present version is this functional governance loop on a live cluster; the remaining comparative, statistical, and scalability limits are explicitly consolidated in Section~10.
