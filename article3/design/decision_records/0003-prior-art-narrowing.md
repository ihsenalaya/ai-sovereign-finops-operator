# ADR 0003

## Title

Supersede ADR 0002's integration-novelty boundary after current industry prior art

## Status

Accepted; supersedes the scientific-novelty and “strongest gap” statements in ADR 0002. ADR 0002 remains as historical provenance.

## Context

The post-update search retained Solo.io quota-management (R55), Keel Budget Envelopes (R56), and AgentBudget (R57). These sources establish in-flight pending spend, reserve-before-dispatch, actual-cost reconciliation, hierarchical budgets, PostgreSQL/gateway enforcement, approvals, and two-phase cost control as prior art. RACER, CONCUR, and Selective Deferred Routing further narrow risk-controlled, constrained, and cost-quality routing claims.

## Decision

Article 3 has only two candidate scientific contributions:

1. a precise problem/safety/event formulation for concurrent unresolved estimated-cost liability under faulty settlement; and
2. a preregistered comparative measurement study against faithful practical envelopes and strong academic baselines.

The operator, gateway, ledger, reservation, routing, and settlement implementation is the reproducible experimental vehicle. No individual integration or algorithmic ingredient is claimed novel.

## Consequences

- every advantage claim is conditional on a frozen operational falsifier and matched-risk evidence;
- null/equivalent practical-envelope results are publishable outcomes and remove superiority wording;
- active design and formal models must preserve unresolved dispatched liability rather than release it on expiry;
- the paper must cite and faithfully compare R55/R56/R57 and cannot use ADR 0002 as a current novelty justification.
